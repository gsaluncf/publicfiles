Case 04 / Performing arts marketing

Theatre Royal Plymouth’s membership campaign

The membership staff at Theatre Royal Plymouth wanted more ticket buyers to join. They used each customer’s booking history to choose a membership level and explain its benefits.

01

Ticket buyers and donors

Theatre staff recorded ticket purchases, attendance, and donations in Tessitura, the theatre’s customer database. Membership support officer Quinn Corvyn and colleagues grouped customers by how often they attended, how much they spent on tickets, and whether they donated. Staff used those groups to select people for a membership offer. Tessitura’s interview with Corvyn.

02

The membership offer

Staff showed each customer two amounts in the invitation. One was the customer’s ticket spending over the previous year. The other was the annual fee for the membership level staff recommended. Tessitura’s account of the offer.

The benefits table lists a £20 Supporter membership and a £50 Friend membership. Both offered early booking. Friend members could also get a discount on two tickets per show. A customer could compare the £50 fee with the discounts available on the tickets they usually bought. Theatre membership benefits published by TRG Arts.

03

Members’ ticket spending

Development manager Erica Vitai led the 2021 relaunch. The theatre invited customers to join by mail and included a reply slip. From July 5 to August 31, 1,106 people joined. The theatre recorded £61,662 in revenue during that period. TRG Arts’ account of the relaunch.

TRG Arts also compared average annual ticket spending across membership levels. Friend members spent 210% more than nonmembers. Staff had used earlier ticket purchases to choose whom to contact. To estimate whether invitations led to additional purchases, staff could compare similar customers who received an invitation with customers who did not.

04

Reporting campaign results

Tessitura published Corvyn’s account of membership growth in March 2026. Membership started at about 1,500, peaked at 5,650, and stood at about 4,800 when he spoke. Each figure counts everyone who held a membership at the time. The 2021 relaunch figure counts only the 1,106 people who joined between July 5 and August 31. Corvyn’s membership figures.

The theatre spent about £8,000 on the relaunch campaign. TRG Arts reported this amount separately from the cost of redesigning the program and buying materials. TRG Arts’ campaign figures.
