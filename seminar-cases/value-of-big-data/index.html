Case 06 / Home energy

The value of Big Data

Bain · Nest thermostat savings

02

A household heating schedule

On early Nest Learning Thermostats, a resident could turn the dial to set a temperature. With Auto-Schedule enabled, the thermostat used repeated adjustments to update the heating schedule. For example, a similar change on two successive weekdays could set the temperature for that time on all five weekdays. The resident could change the setting again. Google Nest’s description of Auto-Schedule.

03

Gas bills before and after installation

In 2015, Nest researchers published an analysis of gas use in 735 homes. They matched utility records to the installation date and accounted for changes in outdoor temperature. They also examined bills from households without a Nest thermostat to account for changes in gas use over the same period. Nest’s study methods.

Before installation, each home used an average of 774 therms of natural gas per year. A therm is a unit of energy. The researchers estimated that heating accounted for 584 of those therms. They estimated average annual savings of 56 therms per home after installation.

04

A homeowner may ask how much gas the household saved on heating. A utility may also want the savings as a share of all gas used in the home. Table 1 contains the figures for both calculations. Nest’s gas and electricity savings table.
