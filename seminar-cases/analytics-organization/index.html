Case 08 / Analytics management

Building an effective analytics organization

McKinsey · Freeport-McMoRan’s Bagdad mill

McKinsey’s article on analytics organizations describes how a central team can share responsibility with an operating department. At Freeport-McMoRan’s copper mine in Bagdad, Arizona, the data scientists could recommend a change. The people who ran the mill had to decide whether to try it.

Copper production at Bagdad

The Bagdad mill grinds ore and separates copper from the surrounding rock. Its managers wanted to increase copper production with the equipment they already had. General manager Justin Cross brought people from the mine, the mill, and the company’s central data science team onto the same project. Freeport-McMoRan and McKinsey’s case account.

02

Operating data and local knowledge

The company had standardized its performance measurements across sites. At Bagdad, the team analyzed three years of mill records. The mill had used one set of operating limits for incoming ore. The team identified seven groups of ore that behaved differently during processing.

McKinsey describes an arrangement in which a central team provides analytical expertise. Staff in each business unit work directly on local decisions. At Bagdad, mill specialists could check whether a proposed setting made sense for the ore they were processing. Building an effective analytics organization.

03

Approval on each shift

The data scientists’ model initially recommended settings twice a day. Metallurgists reviewed them and passed approved changes to shift supervisors and operators. When metallurgists declined a recommendation, they recorded the reason. The team checked those notes against the sensor readings.

One recommendation called for a higher rate of ore processing. Operators had kept a minimum stockpile so the mill would have enough ore to continue running. Executives approved a trial. Mine and crusher staff coordinated the supply while the mill crew increased the processing rate. The October 2018 trial.

04

Reporting mill performance

Throughput measures how much ore the mill processes per day. The recovery rate measures the share of copper in that ore that the mill extracts. Managers used both measurements to assess the trial. Increasing the amount of ore processed could also change the fraction of copper recovered.
